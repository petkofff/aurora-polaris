The brief

A suite of textbook tutors that run in Claude Code. Each one follows a single book lesson by lesson and remembers the learner between sessions. The mark should feel calm, patient and quietly luminous, with no stars or books.

calmpatientluminoussteady

Distinct from edtech clichés (books, caps, owls, stars); recognisable at 16 px; works in one colour; carries the feel of the Aurora mission patch.

Final design

Curtain Wave

One aurora curtain folding across the sky: eleven rays of light that thin as they rise.

  • Aurora without the cliché spikes; tapered rays read as light, not an equalizer
  • Mission-patch palette (teal, violet, pink) on its own night sky
  • 11 rays everywhere; a thickened cut keeps the wave readable at 16 px
