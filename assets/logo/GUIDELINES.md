# Aurora Polaris logo: usage guide

**The idea:** one aurora curtain folding across the night sky. Eleven rays of light thin as they rise,
coloured like the Aurora mission patch: teal at the base, violet in the middle, pink at the tips.

## Files

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `tile.svg` | **Primary icon.** Avatars, app icons, social profiles, anywhere the mark stands alone |
| `tile-small.svg` / `web/favicon.svg` | Icons at 48 px and below (same 11 rays, drawn thicker) |
| `horizontal-light.svg` | Main lockup on white or light pages (README, docs) |
| `horizontal-dark.svg` | Main lockup on dark backgrounds (night or near-black) |
| `stacked-light.svg` / `stacked-dark.svg` | Square-ish spaces: title slides, stickers, posters |
| `symbol-light.svg` / `symbol-dark.svg` | The mark without the name, without a tile |
| `wordmark-light.svg` | The name alone (rarely needed) |
| `one-colour/` | Black, white and flat-violet versions for one-colour printing, embroidery, stamps |
| `web/` | `favicon.ico`, PNG icons, `site.webmanifest`, `head-snippet.html` |
| `png/` | High-resolution PNGs of the main files |
| `presentation.html` | Presentation with mockups |

## Colour

| Name | HEX | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night | `#0A0818` | 10 8 24 | The sky: tile, dark backgrounds, the name on white |
| Teal | `#2BD4C0` | 43 212 192 | Base of the rays (on dark) |
| Violet | `#8B6CF0` | 139 108 240 | Middle of the rays (on dark) |
| Pink | `#FF5C8A` | 255 92 138 | Tips of the rays (on dark) |
| Deep teal | `#0E9686` | 14 150 134 | Base of the rays (on white) |
| Deep violet | `#6A48D8` | 106 72 216 | Middle of the rays (on white) · **one-colour brand colour** |
| Deep pink | `#D9366A` | 217 54 106 | Tips of the rays (on white) |

- The gradient always runs bottom to top: teal → violet (at 45 %) → pink.
- Use the bright set on Night or other dark backgrounds, and the deep set on white. Don't mix them.
- The name is always Night on light backgrounds and white on dark ones. It never takes the gradient.
- On white, all deep colours keep at least a 3:1 contrast ratio (deep teal 3.7, deep pink 4.5, deep violet 5.9).
- For print, ask the printer to match the HEX values. CMYK and Pantone matches haven't been chosen yet.

## Typography

The name is set in **Quicksand SemiBold (600)** (SIL Open Font License) with +10/1000 tracking, all lowercase,
and converted to outlines in every logo file. Its rounded stroke ends match the rounded bases of the rays.
Quicksand is free on Google Fonts and works for headings next to the logo. For body text, use a
plainer sans such as Inter, because Quicksand gets tiring in long passages.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** keep empty space on every side of at least **¼ of the mark's height**.
- **Minimum size:** horizontal lockup 120 px / 25 mm wide; stacked lockup 80 px / 18 mm wide;
  mark alone 24 px. At 48 px and below, use `tile-small.svg` / `symbol-small.svg`.

## Backgrounds

- ✅ White, light grey, Night, near-black.
- ✅ Photos only when they're dark and calm behind the mark. Otherwise use the tile.
- ❌ Mid-tones, saturated colours, busy images. Use the tile or a one-colour version instead.

## Don't

- Change the number of rays, their order or the curve of the wave.
- Recolour the gradient, reverse it (pink at the bottom) or add more colours.
- Add a star, outlines, shadows, glows or bevels.
- Stretch, rotate or skew the mark, or rearrange the lockup.
- Retype the name in another font, or put it in capitals.

## Notes

- These files are generated by a build script kept in a separate logo repository, `aurora-polaris-logo`, along with
  the font. To change the logo, change it there and copy the new files here; don't edit these files by hand.

## Trademark screen (2026-11-21)

A quick web search, **not legal clearance**.

- **"Aurora Polaris" as a whole:** no trademark registration or filing found, and no software, education or tutoring
  brand using the name. The name does appear in unrelated fields:
  - [an unreleased vertical-shooter game on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3659970/Aurora_Polaris/) (the
    nearest, since it's also software);
  - [an Aurora Cannabis facility in Edmonton](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/aurora-cannabis-announces-construction-of-aurora-polaris-300793776.html);
  - an item in a Roblox game, a musician credit and a blog.
- **The words on their own are crowded:**
  - POLARIS is registered by Polaris Industries in many classes, including
    [class 41](https://uspto.report/TM/90573663).
  - [Everway's Polaris](https://www.everway.com/products/polaris/features/) is software for writing special-education
    plans (IEPs).
  - AURORA has many registrations, including an
    [education and entertainment filing in class 41](https://trademarks.justia.com/885/64/aurora-88564419.html).
  - Combined marks are usually judged as a whole, but an examiner might raise these in classes 9, 41 or 42.
- **Not covered:** official database queries (USPTO, EUIPO, national offices), search results outside the US, and
  checking the logo for visual similarity.

**Before you register:**

1. Search "AURORA POLARIS" in [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov) and in
   [TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview), which covers the EU and national offices. Filter classes 9 and 42
   (software) and 41 (education).
2. Search for the logo by image in TMview, and run a reverse image search on `png/tile-1024.png`.
3. Have a trademark attorney review the results before you file.

You don't need to register the name to use it on an open-source project. Registering only lets you stop others
from using it.
